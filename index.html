    Built so neither side gets cheated

    Every buyer is verified

    GST, PAN and bank account checked before a buyer can bid. Buyers who back out lose their bidding rights.

    Money held in escrow

    The buyer's payment is locked before your truck leaves. You never chase a cheque.

    Independent grading

    Moisture, foreign matter and grain size are tested by a third-party assayer. The report is shared with both sides.

    Pickup from your village

    Partner transporters collect from the farm gate. Weight is recorded on a calibrated scale in front of you.
